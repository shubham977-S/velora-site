TRUST & SAFETY

Designed around verified activity, not artificial engagement.

Server-authoritative rewards

The mobile app does not create money or decide final wallet balances. Verified backend events are authoritative.

Fraud controls

Velora is building device, velocity, integrity and provider-signal checks with graduated risk handling rather than blanket blocking.

Transparent status

Incomplete, rejected, duplicate, reversed or non-payable activity does not earn Coins. Live inventory will only appear after approved integrations are enabled.
